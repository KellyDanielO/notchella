Out of the way of your menus

  • The notch no longer covers menus. In apps with a long menu bar, like Android Studio or Xcode, the closed notch shrinks back to the camera housing instead of hiding menu titles such as Window or Help.
  • Menu bar icons stay visible too. Wings and the small activity circles step aside when an icon sits right next to the notch.
  • Quick alerts, like volume, a finished build or an agent that needs you, still show in full. Music, timers and the idle eyes come back as soon as there's room.

Also new

  • Days since your last restart. A small pill in the open notch's header shows how long your Mac has run without a restart or shut down. Hover it to see the date it last started.

Keeping clear of menus needs Accessibility access, which Notchella already uses for the volume and brightness HUD.

Notchella is free and open source.
