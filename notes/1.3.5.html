Codex, live in the notch

  • See Codex working, not just finishing. The notch now shows when Codex starts on a prompt, while it runs tools, and when it's waiting for your approval, like it already does for Claude Code.
  • Turn it on in Settings → Agents & Sleep. Click Install next to Codex hooks, then approve the new hooks in Codex when it asks.
  • Until those hooks are approved, Notchella guesses Codex activity from its CPU use instead of showing nothing.

Notchella is free and open source.
