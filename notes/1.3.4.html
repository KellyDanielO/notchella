Low battery, iPhone style

  • A heads-up at 20% and 10%. When you're on battery, the notch drops down a Low Battery card with how much is left.
  • Low Power Mode in one click. The card's Low Power Mode button turns it on after Touch ID or your password. The battery ring turns yellow while it's on.

Also new

  • Offline and back online. The notch shows when your internet drops and when it comes back. Short blips, like waking from sleep, are ignored. While you're offline, an icon stays in the open notch's header.
  • Turn either one off in Settings → Live activities.
  • Builds of Notchella made by others from the open source code no longer send usage counts.

Notchella is free and open source.
